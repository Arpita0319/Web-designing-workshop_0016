Bhagat Singh

The Revolutionary Freedom Fighter

Bhagat Singh was born on September 28, 1907, in Banga, Punjab, British India. He was deeply influenced by the Jallianwala Bagh massacre and the struggle for Indian independence.

He became a prominent figure in the Indian independence movement, advocating for revolutionary methods to overthrow British rule. Bhagat Singh was involved in several acts of protest and was eventually arrested for his role in the killing of British police officer John Saunders.

  • Born: September 28, 1907
  • Died: March 23, 1931
  • Known for: Indian independence activism
  • Major works: Hindustan Socialist Republican Association
  • Legacy: Bhagat Singh is remembered as a symbol of courage and sacrifice in the fight for India's freedom.
